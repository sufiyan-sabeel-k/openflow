FAQ

How do I sign up or log in?

Use your email address and password. New accounts receive a verification link by email — verify first, then log in. Use Forgot password if you lose access.

Does login work on this demo page alone?

Only after the PHP auth backend is deployed and configured in the site settings. Until then the page shows a backend-required notice and sends nothing.
